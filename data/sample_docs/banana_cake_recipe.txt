Banana Cake
This banana cake recipe uses fresh mashed bananas and buttermilk for super moist results. It was made
for me by a friend while I was visiting her after she had delivered her 11th child. I told her, "I should have
baked for you!"
Submitted by Cindy Carnes 
Prep Time: 30 mins
Cook Time: 1 hr
Additional Time: 45 mins
Total Time: 2 hrs 15 mins
Servings: 18
Yield: 1 (9x13-inch) cake

Ingredients
Cake:
1 ½ cups mashed bananas
2 teaspoons lemon juice
3 cups all-purpose flour
1 ½ teaspoons baking soda
¼ teaspoon salt
2 ⅛ cups white sugar
¾ cup butter
3 eggs
2 teaspoons vanilla extract
1 ½ cups buttermilk
Frosting:
½ cup butter, softened
1 (8 ounce) package cream cheese, softened
1 teaspoon vanilla extract
3 ½ cups confectioners' sugar

Directions
Step 1
Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 275 degrees F (135 degrees C). Grease and flour a 9x13-inch pan.
Step 2
Make cake: Mix mashed bananas with lemon juice in a small bowl.
Step 3
Mix flour, baking soda, and salt in a medium bowl.
Step 4
Cream sugar and butter in a large bowl until light and fluffy, 3 to 4 minutes.
Step 5
Beat in eggs one at a time, then stir in vanilla.
Step 6
Beat in flour mixture alternately with buttermilk. Stir in banana mixture.
Step 7
Pour batter into the prepared pan.
Step 8
Bake in the preheated oven until a toothpick inserted into the center of the cake comes out clean, about 1
hour.
Step 9
Remove cake from the oven and place it directly into the freezer, uncovered, for 45 minutes. This will make
the cake very moist.
Step 10
Meanwhile, make frosting: Cream butter and cream cheese until smooth. Beat in vanilla. Add confectioners
sugar and beat on low speed until combined, then on high until frosting is smooth.
Step 11
Spread frosting on cooled cake.
Step 12
Serve and enjoy!
