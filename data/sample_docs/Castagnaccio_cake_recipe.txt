Castagnaccio - Memorie di Angelina

Castagnaccio

Prep Time: 10 mins 
Cook Time: 20 mins
Total Time: 30 mins

Course: Dessert
Calories: 238.8kcal
Cuisine: Tuscan

Keyword: baked
Servings: 8

Ingredients
250 g chestnut flour
400 ml water
chopped walnuts q.b.
pinoli nuts q.b.
raisins soaked in lukewarm water (or vinsanto) until soft, q.b.
a sprig of fresh rosemary
olive oil
salt
vinsanto (optional)

Instructions
1. In a large mixing bowl, place the flour and a small pinch of salt.
2. Add water bit by bit, along with a drizzle of vinsanto if using, stirring until you have a smooth
batter. Fold in a handful each of the walnuts, pinolis and raisins.
3. Pour the batter into a pie or cake pan or baking dish about 26cm (10-11 in) in diameter.
4. Top with another handful of walnuts and pinolis, along with some fresh rosemary leaves.
Drizzle with olive oil.
5. Bake in a moderate (180C/350F) oven for about 20-25 minutes, or until the batter has cooked
through and little fissures have formed on top.
6. Serve slightly warm or at room temperature.
