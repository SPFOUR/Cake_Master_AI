Onion Cake - Zwiebelkuchen

Prep Time: 20 mins 
Cook Time: 30 mins
Total Time: 1 hr 35 mins

Course: Dinner, Lunch (or Dinner), Party Food
Cuisine: German, Swabia 
Servings: 4 servings
Author: Barbara
  
Ingredients
Dough
250 g flour (= 2 cups)
50 g Butter (a little less than 1/2 stick)
150 ml luke warm warm milk (=1/2 cup and a little more)
1 tsp. yeast
¼ tsp. salt

Filling
500 g onions (2 1/2 large onions)
50 g Butter (a little less than 1/2 stick)
300 g sour cream (1 1/4 cups)
1 tbsp. flour
3 ct. eggs
75 g bacon, cubed (1/4 cup)
some butter, unsalted
some caraway seed (optional)
some salt & pepper to taste

Instructions
Make the dough
1. Add the flour to a large mixing bowl.
2. Add the warm milk and the soft butter.
3. Sprinkel the tsp. of yeast onto the warm milk.
4. Mix shortly, then add the salt.
5. Mix on high speed for 5 minutes.
6. Cover with a tea towel and set aside to a warm place to rise.

Make the filling
1. For the filling cutt and cube the onions. (Keep tissue nearby to dry your tears)
2. Fry the bacon.
3. Optional: Use the butter to fry the onions with the bacon.
Otherwise mix onions, butter, and sour cream in a bowl. Add the fried bacon.
4. Whisk the eggs and add the onion mix. Stir.
5. Add the tbsp. flour and stir again until the mass is nice and smooth.
6. When the dough has about double the size, you can roll it and cover a baking pan on the bottom and rim.
7. Add the onion-egg mix.
8. Sprinkle the bacon on top.
Bake
1. Bake at 425°F for about 30 minutes. Check inbetween and make sure the center is fully
cooked before you serve the Onion Cake.
