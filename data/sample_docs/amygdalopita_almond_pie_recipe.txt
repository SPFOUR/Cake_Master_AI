Amygdalopita (Almond Pie)
Recipe by Peter Minaki
Course: Dessert, Vegetarian
Ingredients
For the syrup
3 cups water
3 cups white sugar
1 cinnamon stick
6 whole cloves
juice of 1/2 lemon
1 shot of brandy
Dry Cake Ingredients
10 Tbsp. medium ground almonds
10 Tbsp. unseasoned breadcrumbs
10 Tbsp. fine semolina flour
zest of 1 orange
1 tsp. ground cinnamon
1/2 tsp. ground clove
1/2 tsp. ground nutmeg
4 tsp. baking powder
Cake Wet Ingredients
10 Tbsp. unsalted butter (room temp)
10 Tbsp. white sugar
Servings
16servings
Prep time
30minutes
Cooking time
45minutes
8 large eggs
1/4 cup brandy
1 tsp. almond extract
Garnish
blanched almond slices
Directions
1 For the  syrup, to a pot medium pot add water, sugar, cinnamon stick, whole cloves, lemon and bring to a
boil. Now simmer on medium heat for 10 minutes. Add the brandy and take off heat and allow to cool.
2 Grease a 13x11 inch pan. Pre-heat your oven to 350F 
3 Add all your dry ingredients in one bowl, stir to combine. 
4 In a large bowl, add the butter and sugar and use your stand mixer (or hand mixer) to whip until off white
and creamy. Now add the eggs one at a time until blended.
5 Add the brandy and almond extract. and combine. 
6 Now add the dry ingredients (in batches) into wet and fold in using a spatula. Pour the batter into your
greased pan, tap a couple of times to remove any air bubbles.
7 Place on the middle rack of your preheated oven and bake for 45 minutes or until a toothpick stuck in the
middle comes out clean.
8 Using a ladle, add cool syrup to just out of the oven cake and allow to cake to set minimum 1 hour. 
9 Cut your Amygdalopita into portions and serve at room temperature topped with almonds and a scoop of
vanilla ice cream. Store in fridge up to a week.
