Gooey Butter Cake
This gooey butter cake has a rich cake mix crust topped with a luscious baked cream cheese layer. It's
super easy to make.
Submitted by barbara 

Prep Time: 10 mins
Cook Time: 35 mins
Total Time: 45 mins
Servings: 18
Yield: 1 (9x13-inch) cake

Ingredients
1 (15.25 ounce) package yellow cake mix
½ cup butter, melted
4 large eggs, divided
2 teaspoons vanilla extract, divided
1 (8 ounce) package cream cheese
4 cups confectioners' sugar

Directions
Step 1
Gather all ingredients and preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
Step 2
Mix together cake mix, butter, 2 eggs, and 1 teaspoon vanilla in a large bowl until well-combined. Pat
mixture into a 9x13-inch dish.
Step 3
Mix cream cheese, remaining 2 eggs, and remaining 1 teaspoon vanilla with an electric mixer in a bowl.
Slowly beat in confectioner's sugar. Pour over cake layer.
Step 4
Bake in the preheated oven until golden brown, 35 to 40 minutes. Cool before serving. Enjoy!
